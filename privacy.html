Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy covers a private Home Assistant installation used by a single household (the "owner"). The only Google account it connects to is the owner's own.

What Google data it accesses

  • Google Drive: only files and folders that the installation itself creates (Google's "drive.file" access). It uses this to store backups of the home automation system.
  • Google Sheets: only the spreadsheet the installation itself creates, to append readings such as room temperatures and energy use.

It does not read, change or delete any other files in the account, and it does not access email, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Data is used only to back up and analyse the owner's own home automation system. Backups are encrypted before upload with a key held only by the owner.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Data stays in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own computer.

Storage and deletion

Files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can remove the installation's access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.

Contact

This installation has no users other than its owner. Questions can be directed to the owner through the Google account that authorised the app.
